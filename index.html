Three moments to try

  1. Before the first investment. Tap "Let's go" and answer the four questions in the phone. You get one plan and a short list of places for the money, with the one that fits your goal already selected. Tap any other to switch.
  2. The first few weeks. Press Next week. Add money, skip a week, open a check-in. Skipping moves the goal date and nothing resets.
  3. The first market fall. Load Arjun, whose 5-year goal sits in the index fund. Press Trigger a market fall, then Next week two or three times. Open Pause or stop. Riya's parking fund barely moves in the same fall.

Concept prototype for a product design assignment on first-time investors aged 20 to 26. Every figure, fee and date is made up for the demo. This is not a real investment product and nothing here is investment advice. In this prototype a month is counted as four weeks.
